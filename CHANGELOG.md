# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-05-19

First release.

- Ask 2–3 targeted questions before giving advice (breed, age, duration, triggers, what's been tried)
- Covers all common dog behaviour issues: barking, biting, pulling, chewing, jumping, anxiety, toilet accidents
- Flags health concerns immediately and directs to a vet before giving any training advice
- Friendly, plain-language tone — no clinical jargon
